FAQs

Good questions.

Can’t find what you’re after? Drop us a message — we’re all ears.

What is IT’S GIVING?

A refreshing soft drink with added benefits, so you no longer need to feel guilty about having a can of pop. It’s made with natural fruit flavours, no artificial sweeteners, is naturally low in sugar and — the absolute bonus — has 6g of fibre to feed all the good bacteria in your gut.

What does ‘gut friendly’ actually mean?

Our ingredients support the health of your gut. We’re feeding all the good bacteria with fibre — 6g to be exact — which helps support digestion, keep you regular (if you know what we mean 😉) and make you less bloated over time.

Where does the fibre come from?

From plants. The fibre comes from chicory root — a natural plant source commonly used for its gut-friendly benefits. We also include a splash of apple cider vinegar, which helps support digestion and rounds out the flavour.

Is it sweet or sour?

It’s just right. We don’t use artificial sweeteners. Instead we use a small amount of stevia — a naturally derived sweetener from plants — to balance the flavour, not overpower it. The real star is the fruit.

There are small bits at the bottom of my can. Is that normal?

Absolutely — that’s the good stuff. Because we use real fruit juice, plant-based fibre and apple cider vinegar, some natural settling happens over time. Give it a very gentle swirl (don’t shake — it’s bubbly!) to mix it back in.

Does it contain caffeine?

No. IT’S GIVING is caffeine-free, so you can enjoy it any time of day.

Is it vegan?

Yes. All of our drinks are made with plant-based ingredients and are suitable for a vegan diet.

Is it OK to have more than one can a day?

With 6g of fibre per can (around 20% of your daily recommended intake) it’s a simple way to top up your day. As always, enjoy in moderation as part of a balanced diet.

Can kids drink it?

IT’S GIVING is suitable for the whole family. As with any product containing fibre, children should enjoy it as part of a balanced diet. For specific health concerns, check with a health professional. If you’re pregnant, breastfeeding or diabetic, we recommend checking with your doctor first.
